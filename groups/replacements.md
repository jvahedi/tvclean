# replacements — apps to install (by hand, not scripted)

| Replace | With | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Google TV home | **Projectivy Launcher** (Play Store) | Install before `apply home`. FLauncher is a minimal open-source alternative. |
| YouTube app ads | **SmartTube** (GitHub releases only) | Needs sideloading allowed. Keep the stock YouTube app; don't disable it. |
| Ad-supported FAST apps | — | Uninstall the ones you don't watch (Haystack, Roku Channel, Pluto, Tubi, CNN, NBC). |
